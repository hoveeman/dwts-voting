Ballroom pulse

Storylines that move votes

Separate signal from noise. These are the early-season developments most likely to shape fantasy value.

01

The scoring reset is real

Derek Hough said the lower starting scores leave room for growth. Guillermo’s 10/30 was the second-lowest score in show history, yet he survived. Don’t compare these totals too literally with prior seasons.

02

Votes beat the scoreboard

Night 1 online voting went down, leaving text votes to determine the elimination. Conner Leavitt went home; Guillermo survived both nights despite the lowest score.

03

Julia has a clean revenge arc

Her “prove Andy Cohen wrong” package landed, Derek told Cohen to “eat it,” and Cohen publicly apologized. It’s a memorable, easy-to-root-for story.

04

Witney brings a rare challenge

The reigning champion is competing while in her second trimester. Guillermo’s partnership brings warmth and a built-in conversation driver.

05

Two ringers, two different risks

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Dewan have the clearest professional dance backgrounds. That raises the technical floor—and the chance of a “too experienced” backlash.

06

Giada exits on the judges’ bottom

Giada De Laurentiis and Alan Bersten were eliminated September 22 after receiving the night’s lowest judges’ score. Thirteen couples advance to Yacht Rock Night.
